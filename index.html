How to export your local catalog

Start the local Skill Atlas server on your laptop, then run this in PowerShell. Keep the exported file on your own device.

Invoke-WebRequest -Uri 'http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/catalog' -OutFile "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\skill-atlas-catalog.json"

Choose that file above. On another device, transfer it privately first. Paths and availability come from the snapshot; this website cannot check your Windows folders.
