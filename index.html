Where do I get my catalog?

On your laptop, start the local Skill Atlas, then export your catalog in PowerShell:

Invoke-WebRequest -Uri 'http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/catalog' -OutFile "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\skill-atlas-catalog.json"

Open that file here. To use your phone, transfer it privately first.

Your catalog stays in this tab and clears when you reload. Only your theme and shortlist IDs are saved. This site cannot check your laptop folders.
